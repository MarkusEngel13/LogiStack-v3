- **VPIP**: puts money in voluntarily before the flop
- **PFR**: raises before the flop
- **Limp**: limps when nobody has raised (open limp or overlimp)
- **3-bet**: re-raises a single raise
- **F 3B**: folds to a 3-bet after raising
- **CB F**: preflop raiser bets the flop when he can
- **CB T**: bets the turn again after a flop c-bet, when he can
- **CB R**: bets the river again after the turn, when he can
- **F CB**: folds to a flop c-bet
- **R CB**: raises a flop c-bet
- **Lead**: bets into the preflop raiser on the flop (donk)
- **F ≤⅓**: folds to a first bet of up to 40 % pot (any street after the flop)
- **F ½-¾**: folds to a first bet of 40-80 % pot
- **F ≥pot**: folds to a first bet of 80 % pot or more
- **XR**: check-raises when he checks and someone bets
- **WTSD**: goes to showdown after seeing the flop
- **W$SD**: wins (some of) the pot at showdown
